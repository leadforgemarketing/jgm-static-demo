PR photography and corporate photography are often used interchangeably to describe creative and professional photography that is used for marketing and promotional purposes. There is a subtle difference between the two terms.

PR photography generally refers to photos that are taken of public figures or celebrities in order to generate positive publicity. Corporate photography usually refers to photos that are taken of business people or employees in order to promote the company or brand.
