Frequently asked questions

As a London event photographer, I’ve captured various sizes and styles of events across the city. From small internal events for a single organisation, to large-scale awards ceremonies with multiple stages and speakers over several days. I’ve worked in historic buildings such as The Savoy.

My aim is for the event to run as if I am not there. I use cameras with a silent shutter system so no one gets distracted and I don’t draw attention to myself during quieter moments. I photograph the speakers, audiences and interactions between people without staging or influencing them at all.
