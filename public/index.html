Frequently asked questions

As a London event photographer, I’ve captured various sizes and styles of events across the city. From small internal events for a single organisation, to large-scale awards ceremonies with multiple stages and speakers over several days. I’ve worked in historic buildings such as The Savoy.

My aim is for the event to run as if I am not there. I use cameras with a silent shutter system so no one gets distracted and I don’t draw attention to myself during quieter moments. I photograph the speakers, audiences and interactions between people without staging or influencing them at all. I can take posed group photos if needed, but I’ll get the most authentic overview of the event when people just enjoy themselves and don’t feel forced to smile.

I’m based in Tooting, so the majority of my work is as a London event photographer, however I’m more than happy to travel anywhere in the UK. I have a very streamlined and simple setup, so can travel easily across the city on public transport, or on foot. Equally, I’m happy to travel throughout the UK, and my work occasionally takes me across Europe too.

Areas served in London: Camden, Chelsea, City of London, Hackney, Islington, Kensington, Lambeth, Southwark, Tower Hamlets, Wandsworth, Westminster.

I work quickly to edit and deliver every job, so images are ready for social media and online use as soon as you need them. Headshots are typically delivered within 48 hours. Event galleries are returned promptly — often the next day when the brief requires it.

I have been a professional event photographer in London for over ten years, and I set up my own business 13 years ago.
