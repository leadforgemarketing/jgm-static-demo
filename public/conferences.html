Frequently asked questions

As a London conference photographer, I’ve captured various sizes and styles of conference across the city. From small internal conferences for a single organisation, to large-scale conferences with multiple stages and speakers over several days.

I’ve worked in historic buildings, The Savoy, Australia House, the NEC and even the Emirates Stadium, so I’m used to adapting to my environment to get the most powerful photographs of a conference in any setting.

My aim is for the conference to run as if I am not there. I use cameras with a silent shutter system so no one gets distracted and I don’t draw attention to myself during quieter moments. I photograph the speakers, audiences and interactions between people without staging or influencing them at all.

I’m based in Tooting, so the majority of my work is as a London conference photographer, however I’m more than happy to travel anywhere in the UK. My work occasionally takes me across Europe too.

I have a number of videographers that I work with on a regular basis who offer this service.
