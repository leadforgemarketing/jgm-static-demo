Hello, I’m James

A professional London corporate photographer, specialising in events and headshots.

Hi, I’m James, a professional London corporate photographer, specialising in event photography, conference photography and corporate headshots.

I have spent the last 15 years working in the photography industry, and I set up my own business 13 years ago. My base is in Wimbledon, and I travel across the city and further afield around the whole of the UK and Europe, capturing professional images for a range of business and private clients.

Documentary-style photography is what I’m particularly known for, working discreetly so that the atmosphere and energy of the moment is captured in my images. I work quickly to edit and deliver every job, so images are ready for social media and online use as soon as you need them.

My experience in the industry is hugely varied. I have a large number of clients that I work with on a regular basis, as well as being commissioned for high-profile events at prestigious venues. As a people person, I’m happiest when I’ve got my camera in my hand and can get immersed in documenting awesome events and conference photography. I also really enjoy putting people at ease and capturing professional corporate headshots in London using my mobile studio.

If you have any questions or would be interested in a quote, please do get in touch — I’d love to hear from you.
